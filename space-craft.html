Physics Principle: Gravity Effect on Flame Spread

In 0g microgravity (Grashof number Gr ≈ 0), buoyancy stops and hot air no longer rises. Flames form small spherical blue diffusion balls with slow burn rates. Microgravity Combustion & Materials
NASA PSI-26: Burning and Suppression of Solids (BASS) PSI Repository

ISS flight experiment measuring low-speed airflow effects on solid fuel burning. Provided Critical Oxygen Index (LOI) and flame spread velocities for Nomex HT90-40, Delrin, PMMA, and Kapton used in this simulation.

NASA Saffire-I through VI (NTRS 20240002981) NTRS Citation

Full-scale spacecraft fire experiments conducted on Cygnus cargo resupply vehicles. Provided real-world Heat Release Rates (HRR), toxic CO/CO₂ yields, and optical smoke obscuration (OD/m) models.

NASA PSI-69 & NTRS 20150023456: Flame Extinguishment (FLEX) FLEX Tests

Evaluated carbon dioxide (CO₂) and helium suppressant dilution limits across 284 test points on the ISS, discovering invisible low-temperature (~500°C) cool-flame regimes in microgravity.

NASA-STD-6001B (Test 1 & Upward Flammability) NASA Standard

Official NASA standard test procedure evaluating material flammability, odor, off-gassing, and upward flame propagation across habitable spacecraft compartments.

Battery Chemistry & Thermal Runaway
FAA Fire Safety Branch (Report DOT/FAA/TC-15/59) FAA Report

Thermal runaway propagation dynamics of Lithium-Ion 18650/21700 NMC cells. Establishes the 160°C cathode breakdown temperature, self-oxidizing decomposition (LOI = 0%), and explosive hydrogen/HF off-gassing.

NASA JSC 20793: Crewed Space Vehicle Battery Safety JSC Standard

Guidelines for spacecraft battery containment, cell-to-cell thermal isolation, emergency high-rate pressure relief vents, and mandatory compartment isolation upon thermal runaway onset.

Thornton's Rule of Fire Safety (Combustion Energetics) Empirical Constant

Governs compartment oxygen depletion: net heat of combustion per unit mass of oxygen consumed is universally 13.1 ± 0.7 MJ per kg of O₂ consumed across hydrocarbon and polymer materials.
