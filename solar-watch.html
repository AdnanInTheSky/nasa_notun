NASA DONKI (Space Weather Database of Notifications, Knowledge, Information) DONKI API

Provides the real solar flare catalog, peak flux measurements ($W/m^2$), and active region source locations tracked by NASA GSFC and the Community Coordinated Modeling Center (CCMC).

NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC Scales R, S, G) NOAA SWPC

Defines the quantitative thresholds for Radio Blackouts ($R1\text{--}R5$ based on GOES X-ray flux), Solar Radiation Storms ($S1\text{--}S5$ for proton flux >10 MeV), and Geomagnetic Storms ($G1\text{--}G5$ Kp index).

Parker Solar Probe & Parker Spiral Magnetic Field Model Parker Mission

Archimedean spiral magnetic topology created by solar rotation. Explains why solar flares on the Sun's western limb ($20^\circ\text{W}\text{--}60^\circ\text{W}$) funnel relativistic solar protons directly to Earth and lunar orbits.

NASA NTRS 20130013897 & JSC-65995 (Astronaut Radiation Shielding) NASA NTRS

Deep-space habitat radiation shelter baselines. Demonstrates that polyethylene and onboard water walls provide optimal shielding against solar particle events during transit and surface missions.
